# PROJ-010 triage, round 2 — 61 open items, and a closure path

**2026-08-23.** Supersedes `TRIAGE-2026-08-22.md` (53 items) — that pass stands, this one re-runs it
against three things that changed since: **PROJ-011 and PROJ-012 now exist as homes**, SPEC-092 was
**cancelled**, and today's audits added items.

## The headline: PROJ-010 is one small spec away from closeable

Its brief names five success signals. **Four are met.** The fifth was two clauses; the SPEC-092
clause is withdrawn (cancelled 2026-08-23), leaving **one open dependency: the strict-JSON
`escape_json` tail.**

⚠ **And that item may not be what its label says.** It is filed as *"a deliberate behaviour change
to fix a correctness issue"* — but **RFC 8259 §7 requires escaping only `"`, `\`, and U+0000–U+001F.**
`0x7F` and the C1 range (U+0080–U+009F) are **legal unescaped**, so the current implementation
(`src/cli/report.rs:116`) is already spec-compliant. It is **hardening, not a defect.**
**Decide which, because it is the last thing between PROJ-010 and a clean close:** ship it as a
small hardening spec, or amend the signal the way the SPEC-092 clause was amended. **Either closes
the project. Doing neither keeps it open by accident** — which is exactly what SPEC-092 was doing.
